UniOps — Privacy Policy

Effective 23 July 2026 · Canada Royal Milk

1. Who we are

UniOps is an internal business application built and operated by Canada Royal Milk ("we", "us") for its own employees. It is not offered to the public, not listed on any app marketplace, and has no external customers. This policy describes how UniOps handles data when it connects to a QuickBooks Online company that Canada Royal Milk itself owns.

2. What data we access

With the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope, UniOps reads accounts payable records from our own QuickBooks Online company:

  • Vendor master records (name, contact details, account number, terms)
  • Vendor bills and bill payments, including line items and amounts
  • Vendor credits and non-bill purchases
  • Chart of accounts
  • Attachments linked to the above records

We do not request the payments, payroll, or OpenID profile scopes. UniOps does not read customer or employee personal data from QuickBooks Online.

3. Why we access it

Canada Royal Milk is migrating its accounts payable records out of QuickBooks Online and into UniOps. The connection exists to copy our own historical accounting data into our own system. It is a one-time migration with occasional manually triggered incremental pulls, not a continuous background sync.

4. Write access

UniOps reads only. It does not create, modify, or delete any record in QuickBooks Online.

5. Where data is stored

Retrieved data is stored in Canada Royal Milk's own PostgreSQL database on company infrastructure located in Canada. Access is restricted to authorized finance and IT personnel through UniOps' role-based permissions. Data is transmitted over TLS.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or machine learning. It is used solely for Canada Royal Milk's internal accounting and reporting.

7. Credentials

OAuth tokens are stored outside version control with restricted file permissions and are never written to logs or source code. Refresh tokens are rotated by Intuit and the latest value replaces the previous one on every refresh.

8. Retention

Migrated accounting records are retained for the period required by Canadian bookkeeping and tax-record obligations. When the QuickBooks Online connection is no longer needed, we revoke the authorization and delete the stored tokens.

9. Disconnecting

An administrator of our QuickBooks Online company can revoke UniOps' access at any time from Settings → Manage Users → Apps, or at appcenter.intuit.com. Revocation immediately stops all further access.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.
